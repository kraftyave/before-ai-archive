### Exhibit 1 — Handwritten Letters
> A still-life arrangement of vintage handwritten letters on aged paper, elegant cursive script visible through translucent sheets, wax seal impressions in deep red, warm natural window light, shallow depth of field, tilted angles, shot on medium format film

### Exhibit 2 — Love Letters
> An open vintage love letter on cream paper, elegant flowing handwriting, a pressed dried flower resting on the page, wax seal in burgundy, soft golden hour light streaming diagonally across the paper, romantic mood, shallow depth of field, cinematic composition
