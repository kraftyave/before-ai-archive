The Analog Era — c. 300,000 BCE – 2022 CE

The Human
Archive

Preserving what we made before the machines learned to think

Browse the collection

Before the machines learned to think, humans made things with their hands. They wrote letters on paper, wound their watches, and got lost without asking for directions.

This is an archive of what we almost lost. A museum of artifacts from the analog era — each one carrying the weight of human intention, imperfection, and care.

We preserve these objects not because they are useful, but because they are evidence of who we were.

— Curatorial Statement
